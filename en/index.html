Fully automated media production

Your brand becomes a Media Machine.

84 Tainment builds and runs media brands that produce, distribute and monetize their content fully automatically. Instead of single videos, a system emerges: a content infrastructure that runs around the clock — from the idea through production to international distribution on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

The same mechanics we used to scale our own six brands into 100+ countries, we make available to companies, agencies and creators. Three steps carry everything: Automate, Scale, Monetize.

Automated — processes that run themselves.

Automation is the core of everything we do. We take the recurring marketing, video and business processes behind a brand and cast them into systems that run without manual input. The result: consistent quality, predictable output and a team that focuses on strategy instead of busywork.

  1. 01

    Marketing automation

    Campaigns, email flows, social posting and reporting run rule-based and on their own — from the first touchpoint to conversion.

  2. 02

    Video marketing automation

    Editing, subtitles, thumbnails, upload and video SEO become an automated assembly line — one shoot turns into dozens of published assets.

  3. 03

    Business automation

    The operational workflows behind every brand — from the content pipeline to recurring tasks — run as one continuous, automated system.

Scaled — from one market to the whole world.

Reach is no accident — it's system work. We bring brands to millions of views on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram and export proven formats automatically into new languages and countries. What works in one market runs in the next shortly after — without reinventing production.

  1. 01

    Reach on YouTube, TikTok & Instagram

    Platform-native formats and consistent distribution bring brands to millions of views — data-driven and repeatable.

  2. 02

    International across 100+ countries

    Translation, localization and re-distribution run automatically — the same content opens up new languages and markets.

  3. 03

    Production & distribution automated

    Scripting, the production phase and downstream video marketing — upload, SEO articles, email marketing — mesh together as one continuous machine.

Monetized — set it up once, earn for the long run.

Reach is only half the battle — it has to pay off. We turn video content into predictable revenue: through products, courses, memberships and ad income. The key is the principle behind it: content is set up once and keeps earning afterwards — as largely passive income instead of constant extra effort.

  1. 01

    Set it up once, earn long-term

    Content and sales systems built once keep working while you no longer touch anything manually.

  2. 02

    Products, courses & memberships

    From real reach and a community that knows you, offers emerge with repeat-purchase and subscription potential.

  3. 03

    Passive income from content

    Ad revenue, affiliate and own products flow together across all platforms and countries — automatically analyzed and optimized.
